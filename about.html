Our History

Sixteen Years of Proven Operational Reliability

Established in 2010, Too Fast Transport LLC has spent over 16 years providing reliable heavy machinery, certified plant hire, and specialized civil logistics for major construction projects throughout the United Arab Emirates.

Our expertise is positioned where every landmark project begins: in the ground. Before high-rise towers, commercial developments, and industrial facilities can rise, our fleet executes the essential groundwork, deep basement excavation, trenching, soil clearance, and foundation drilling support that civil engineers rely upon.

We believe client relationships thrive on clear communication and dependable follow-through. Every project receives dedicated operations support, certified equipment operators, and live vehicle tracking to ensure seamless execution from initial site mobilization to final handover.

Ground Engineering & Substructure Works

Tower Foundation, Deep Excavation & Piling Support Across the UAE

Before modern high-rise towers and commercial complexes rise across Dubai, Sharjah, and Abu Dhabi, Too Fast Transport provides the earthmoving muscle, piling rig logistics, and deep excavation equipment that establish solid ground foundations.

Deep Basement & Foundation Excavation

Executing bulk earth excavation, deep basement digging, and trenching for high-rise towers and civil developments. Our fleet of heavy excavators, rock breakers, and JCB backhoes removes soil down to required foundation levels with speed and precision.

  • ✓ Deep basement pit digging & bedrock trenching
  • ✓ Bulk soil removal & muck-away disposal
  • ✓ Continuous site clearance for foundation teams

Piling, Drilling & Shoring Rig Support

Dedicated equipment logistics for bored piling, secant wall drilling, contiguous piles, and sheet piling contractors. We haul and maneuver heavy augers, drilling buckets, tremie pipes, casing oscillators, and bentonite supplies directly on site.

  • ✓ Piling rig mobilization on heavy lowbed trailers
  • ✓ Shoring wall & anchor drilling support
  • ✓ Tremie pipes, augers, and casing line transport

Sub-Base Grading, Leveling & Compaction

Accurate ground leveling, subgrade compaction, and aggregate base preparation ensure optimal load-bearing strength before pouring concrete raft foundations. Our wheel loaders and graders construct firm working pads and site access routes.

  • ✓ Laser accurate subgrade leveling & land grading
  • ✓ Crushed aggregate backfilling & roller compaction
  • ✓ Solid crane staging pads & site haul roads

Mobile Crane Lifting for Foundation Rigging

High-tonnage mobile cranes and boom trucks engineered for lifting into deep foundation trenches. We handle the placement of heavy rebar steel cages into pile boreholes, sheet pile extraction, vibro hammer rigging, and dewatering pump positioning.

  • ✓ Piling rebar steel cage lowering into boreholes
  • ✓ Sheet pile driving & vibro hammer rigging
  • ✓ Heavy dewatering pumps & discharge piping placement
