Heavy Material Lifting to High Elevations and Rooftops in UAE

Lifting high-tonnage industrial equipment, heavy structural modules, or placing mechanical units onto high rooftops demands heavy telescopic mobile cranes, verified load charts, and certified operators. Too Fast Transport provides high-capacity all-terrain mobile cranes up to 200 tons capacity engineered for demanding job sites across the United Arab Emirates.

Whether hoisting high-tonnage HVAC chillers to building roofs, erecting heavy structural steel trusses, placing prefabricated bridge girders, or extracting deep foundation casing tools, our crane fleet delivers reliable hydraulic reach, multi-fall hoist ropes, and computerized load moment indicators (LMI) for total precision.
