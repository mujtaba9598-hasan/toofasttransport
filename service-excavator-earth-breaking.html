Specialized Excavation, Rock Breaking & Structural Demolition

In construction and civil infrastructure, excavation and earth breaking represent a specialized department requiring dedicated heavy machinery, robust attachments, and skilled operators. Whether breaking solid rock strata, demolishing concrete boundary walls, fracturing retaining structures, or executing mass foundation digging, Too Fast Transport deploys tracked hydraulic excavators engineered for severe-duty site operations.

Our excavator fleet features dual operating modes: high-frequency hydraulic rock breakers for crushing hard rock, breaking masonry, and demolishing concrete structures, alongside high-capacity digging buckets for rapid soil trenching, basement excavation, and mass spoil loading into haulage dump trucks.

Supplied With Certified Heavy Equipment Operators: Every excavator deployment includes trained, licensed operators proficient in rock chisel angle control, precision wall demolition without structural collateral damage, and strict UAE municipal site safety standards.

Core Operations Handled by Our Excavator Fleet

From hard rock fracturing to full structural demolition, our machines are built for demanding UAE site conditions.

01

Hydraulic Rock & Bedrock Breaking

Equipped with high-impact hydraulic hammers for fracturing tough limestone, solid bedrock formations, and compacted conglomerate ground layers during foundation preparation.

02

Concrete Wall & Structure Demolition

Precision demolition of boundary walls, reinforced concrete partitions, old villas, foundation footings, and retaining structures with controlled drop zones and zero debris spill.

03

Deep Basement & Foundation Excavation

Mass earthmoving for multi-level commercial and residential basements, pile cap uncovering, shoring wall perimeters, and geotechnical ground engineering.

04

Deep Utility Trenching

High-efficiency trenching for deep sewerage mains, high-voltage DEWA/SEWA cable trenches, chilled water district cooling pipelines, and telecommunication conduits.

05

Dump Truck Spoil Loading

High-speed cycle loading of excavated soil, rock fragments, and demolition rubble onto 20-ton and 32-ton tipper dump trucks for prompt disposal to municipality dump sites.

06

Site Levelling & Sub-Base Cut

Precision grade cuts, ramp shaping, embankment grading, and plot preparation for industrial warehouses, logistics yards, and commercial construction plots.
