Overview

Engineered Slide-Bed Ground Loading Capabilities

Transporting disabled machinery, low ground clearance access platforms, or heavy plant equipment without crane loading bays requires specialized carriers. Too Fast Transport operates modern hydraulic tilt tray carriers that incline smoothly down to ground level.

Equipped with high capacity hydraulic recovery winches, our tilt trailers can pull inoperable forklifts, rollers, road pavers, or commercial vehicles with smooth winch control. The ultra low approach angle eliminates chassis scraping and underside damage.

Each carrier features heavy duty anchor points, high strength chain binders, and pneumatic air suspension to guarantee complete stability during highway transit across Dubai, Sharjah, Abu Dhabi, and the Northern Emirates.
