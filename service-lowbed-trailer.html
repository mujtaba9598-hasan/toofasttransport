Heavy Machinery Shifting Up to 150 Tons Across the UAE

Transporting high-tonnage construction excavators, geotechnical drilling rigs, and heavy industrial plant equipment demands precision engineering, certified drivers, and specialized multi-axle lowbed trailers. Too Fast Transport maintains an extensive fleet of lowbed carriers capable of handling single equipment loads up to 150 tons anywhere within the United Arab Emirates.

Our trailers feature reinforced goosenecks, hydraulic widening decks, heavy-duty outriggers, and low loading angles designed specifically for heavy tracked equipment. Coupled with high-output Mercedes-Benz Actros and Volvo 6x4 tractor units, we ensure stable highway transit along the E311, E611, and major Abu Dhabi Western Region arterial routes.

All equipment transport is executed with certified machine operators and trained licensed drivers. We manage site route clearances, RTA oversize transit permissions, and coordinated police escorts for over-width and over-length heavy assets.

Machinery Shifting Profiles

Specialized Heavy Equipment Handled by Our Lowbed Fleet

Tailored multi-axle configurations for major UAE infrastructure, earthmoving, and civil plant assets.

Tracked Excavators & Breakers

Safe mobilization of 20-ton to 90-ton crawler excavators (CAT, Komatsu, Volvo, Hitachi) equipped with rock breakers and heavy foundation buckets. Secured with four-point certified lashing chains and heavy chocking.

Piling & Drilling Rigs

Transport of heavy rotary drilling rigs (Bauer, Casagrande, Soilmec), shoring wall drillers, continuous flight augers (CFA), and hydraulic power units to deep basement construction sites across Dubai and Abu Dhabi.

Bulldozers & Wheel Loaders

Multi-axle lowbed transit for large Caterpillar D8/D9 bulldozers, high-tonnage wheel loaders, and site motor graders with outrigger extensions for wide blade clearance on UAE highway networks.

Crawler Crane Tracks & Carbodies

Tonnage-rated transport for crawler crane undercarriages, modular track sideframes (Sany, Kobelco, Liebherr), and centralized carbody assemblies between staging yards and active infrastructure zones.

Crushing & Screening Plants

Low-angle ramp loading for mobile track-mounted jaw crushers, cone crushers, and vibratory screening plants operating in quarry and aggregate recycling operations across Sharjah, Ras Al Khaimah, and Fujairah.

Industrial Power & Transformers

Heavy-tonnage movement of electric utility transformers, diesel generator power plants, and industrial autoclave cylinders requiring low gravity center positioning and zero vibration transit.
