Engineered Flatbed Haulage for UAE Infrastructure & Industrial Projects

Transporting high-tonnage industrial components, oversized casing pipes, or critical foundation machinery demands certified prime movers, heavy multi-axle trailers, and experienced lashing crews. Too Fast Transport operates a modern fleet of 40ft heavy flatbed trailers, lowbeds, and support pickup vehicles built to satisfy the strictest UAE civil and industrial transport specifications.

Our fleet features powerful European 6x4 tractor units (Volvo and Mercedes-Benz Actros) engineered for heavy-duty highway corridors, including Sheikh Mohammed Bin Zayed Road (E311), Emirates Road (E611), and the Abu Dhabi-Western Region industrial routes (E11 / Ruwais). Specialized Cargo Solutions

Engineered Haulage for High-Value Industrial Freight

Proven equipment profiles and custom rigging configurations tailored to UAE enterprise cargo.

Steel Casing & Pipelines

Heavy-duty haulage of large-diameter steel pipes, welded casing tubes, and oilfield line pipes. Secured using vertical steel stanchions, timber dunnage, and multi-point tension straps to eliminate transit rolling.

Crane Lattice Booms & Ballast

Specialized long-overhang transport of mobile and crawler crane lattice boom sections, fly jibs, and modular high-tonnage counterweight blocks directly between staging yards and active construction zones.

Deep Foundation & Piling Tooling

Secure mobilization of telescopic kelly bars, rotary drilling shafts, tremie concrete pipes, and hydraulic powerpack modules with heavy chocking, warning banners, and safety escort support.

Precast Concrete & Structural Slabs

Vibration-cushioned transit of prefabricated concrete wall panels, foundation slabs, bridge girders, and precast civil modules with dedicated rubber protection to prevent edge chipping and stress fractures.

Structural Steel & Rebar Bundles

High-tonnage highway transport of 12-meter straight reinforcement rebar bundles, structural H-beams, and fabricated steel trusses delivered directly to jobsite laydown yards on strict delivery windows.

Containers & Industrial Machinery

Twist-lock certified haulage for 20ft and 40ft ISO shipping containers, alongside crated mechanical plant modules, high-capacity air compressors, industrial generators, and electrical transformers.
